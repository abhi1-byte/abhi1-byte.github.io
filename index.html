Who I Am

I am originally from India, currently based in the United States and pursuing a graduate degree in Cloud Computing at Indiana Wesleyan University. My technical foundation is in backend development, built through an intensive self-study period focused on Java, Spring Boot, REST APIs, Spring Security, and JWT-based authentication. I enjoy solving structured problems and building systems that are reliable, secure, and maintainable.

What I Am Working Toward

My current focus is expanding into full-stack development by building JavaScript and React skills on top of my existing backend experience. The goal is to connect a React frontend to my Spring Boot backend and deploy the complete application on AWS, producing a portfolio-quality project that demonstrates end-to-end development and cloud deployment capability.
